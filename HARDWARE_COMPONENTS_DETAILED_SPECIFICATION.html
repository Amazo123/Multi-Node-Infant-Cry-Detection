Capacitor Code Clarification: A 100 nF (0.1 µF) capacitor is stamped 104 (10 × 104 pF = 100,000 pF = 100 nF = 0.1 µF). A capacitor stamped 103 is only 10 nF (0.01 µF), which is 10 times too small. Always use 104 for power decoupling and ADC noise filtering.

Cot Node Strapping Pin Note: GPIO 9 controls the ESP32-C3 boot mode. Our Blue LED design (3.3V → 330 Ω → Anode → Cathode → GPIO 9) naturally pulls the pin HIGH during power-up, ensuring normal SPI flash boot. Do not short GPIO 9 directly to ground on power-on or the chip will enter ROM download mode.
